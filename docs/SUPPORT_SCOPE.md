# CDD — bounded support and assisted interpretation policy

**Published:** 16 October 2026  
**Provider:** Razorglint Labs / TCOG Collective LLC  
**Scope:** proposed service limits for **new** CDD self-service subscription purchases, once clearly disclosed at checkout or in an accepted agreement.

This policy clarifies the human services included with each monthly subscription. It does **not** expand the license rights, change current advertised subscription prices, or retroactively amend existing customers' agreed terms. A customer-specific signed agreement controls if different. Before new sales, the Stripe product/checkout description and pre-purchase terms **must match** this policy.

## Monthly included human support — hard limits

| Tier | Subscription | Written support requests per paid billing month | Included focused report interpretations | Maximum combined human work per paid billing month |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Starter | **$49/month** | **2** | **1** | **20 minutes** |
| Team | **$149/month** | **4** | **1** | **45 minutes** |
| Consultant | **$299/month** | **6** | **2** | **90 minutes** |

**All included report interpretations are part of, not additional to, the support-request and time limits.** Example: a Starter customer's one interpretation uses one of two requests and shares the 20-minute total budget with the remaining request. A Consultant's allowance applies **per subscribing account**, not once per client engagement.

### What counts as a request

- **One request** is one focused question or one reproducible technical issue sent to **razorglint.ops@protonmail.com**. Up to one related clarification may be handled within the same request **as long as the time budget remains**. A separate issue or another report counts as another request.
- **One assisted interpretation** means one written explanation of a **single focused question about one already generated, appropriately redacted CDD report**. To keep the scope bounded, send a summary/excerpt covering **up to 10 policy claims or five pages**; large reports must be narrowed to a specific section. We do not inspect original evidence records or independently evaluate regulatory compliance.
- Time budgets apply **in aggregate**, across receiving/reviewing input, reproducing a support issue, composing replies and explaining reports. Reasonably recorded staff working time is counted and the customer is notified when their allotment is exhausted.
- Support is **asynchronous email**, ordinarily during business working days, on a **best-effort** basis. There is **no guaranteed response or resolution time**, weekend/on-call coverage, 24/7 monitoring, or contractual service-level agreement. Team's published **priority support** means preferential queue ordering over Starter **when capacity permits**, not a guaranteed deadline; Consultant inquiries are similarly triaged by workload.
- Unused requests, interpretations and human-service minutes **expire at the end of each paid billing month**. They have no cash value and do not roll forward.
- The **7-day Starter trial, when offered, covers self-service evaluation only**. It does not include human report interpretation or the paid-month support allowance until the first successful subscription payment, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

### Excluded work and paid add-ons

The subscription does **not** include live calls, screen-share or remote desktop sessions, onsite assistance, custom integrations, deployment/installation at a customer's site, evidence collection or classification, policy drafting, custom template development, professional audit work, legal/regulatory advice, investigation of large datasets, guaranteed fixes, or unrestricted report-by-report review.

At the cap, Razorglint may offer separately quoted assistance **only after explicit written customer approval**. **No automatic overage fees or implied obligation to continue support.** Customers can also submit a new request during their next paid billing month.

Customers should submit only synthetic or properly redacted, authorized materials. Do **not** email credentials, raw production logs, sensitive personal information or unredacted customer files. Responsible security vulnerability reports can always be submitted via [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md); accepting a security report does not guarantee a resolution SLA or create unlimited individual support.

### Subscription updates versus human support

The license may provide access to product fixes, documentation and templates **when they are published during an active subscription**, but **no fixed monthly engineering or template release cadence is guaranteed for new purchases**. Access to product updates is distinct from the bounded human-service allowance.

### Rights and precedence

These caps apply only to the services included under the newly disclosed self-service tiers. They **do not** authorize customer redistribution, embedding/OEM usage, white-label rights or sublicense grants. Enterprise/OEM support levels, deployment rights, pricing and availability require separate written agreements. See [LICENSE_TIERS.md](../LICENSE_TIERS.md) and [TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md](../TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md).

**Prior-customer safeguard:** Existing subscribers' original checkout promises and accepted terms must be checked and honored as applicable; obtain appropriate notice/consent before a material reduction in agreed benefits. A GitHub edit alone does not alter an existing customer's contract.
