# CDD commercial decision — LICENSING FIRST
**Decision:** 2026-10-14 · **Authority:** Damian / TCOG Collective LLC / Razorglint Labs
**Effective for:** October 15 outreach and subsequent commercial material
**Supersedes:** October 4 Sale 01 outright asset-sale recommendation, €29,500 opening ask, €20–25k close target and €15k transfer floor as *active outreach instructions*. Retain those only as historical decision records.

## Ownership and default deal
**Keep ComplianceDriftDetector source/IP ownership. License capability and usage rights; do not solicit an outright sale.**

Primary routes, in order of ease of validation:
1. **Existing self-service subscription licenses:** currently documented Starter $49/mo, Team $149/mo and Consultant $299/mo (verify live checkout/customer obligations before making operational promises).
2. **B2B/team licensing or paid evaluations:** narrowly scoped internal-use deployment with paid support/integration separately specified.
3. **OEM / embedding / white-label license:** non-exclusive by default, with precise product, use-case, deployment, territory, term, redistribution and reporting boundaries. No automatic source-code rights, sublicensing or future IP.
4. **Strategic licensing partners:** GRC platforms, assurance firms, boutique vCISO/consultancies and audit-readiness integrators.

**Prefer renewable/annual contracts and paid pilots where they fit.** Do not claim recurring revenue until independently confirmed.

## Commercial guardrails
- CDD ownership remains with Razorglint/TCOG. Any source access is exceptional and under a separate source escrow / controlled-access contract; a standard license gives no assignment.
- No exclusive license, perpetual worldwide rights, unlimited seats/clients, redistribution or derivative rights by default. Price those concessions separately if desired.
- Prestige Forge / Prestige-Reforged, Viventia, SAR, VEAC, PolicySourceEngine and all other IP stay outside the contract. No cross-license implied.
- Do not quote the **€29,500 old sale ask**, €20–25k target or €15k floor to licensing prospects. These are superseded as operating terms, not valid OEM prices or market valuations.
- B2B/OEM prices: **discovery/quotation required**, based on scope; no invented flat list price.
- Do not promise live monitoring, automatic policy reading, production certification, authenticated original-source evidence or SaaS maturity.
- No unlimited founder services. Support, maintenance, upgrades, integration and liability must have separate written boundaries.
- Maintain a lead ledger: prospect, segment, pilot/licensing interest, economics, objections, next step and renewal potential.

## Outright sale: exceptional contingency only
Enter a *separate ownership-sale review* if:
1. A credible buyer presents a genuinely compelling offer reflecting **future license and OEM upside, IP control, strategic scarcity, alternative opportunities, diligence and transfer costs**; **or**
2. A bounded, evidence-based licensing test fails to show commercial traction, and Damian deliberately authorizes reconsidering an outright sale.

No automatic sale trigger, no public `for sale` language, and **no pre-authorization** to accept the former €29,500 price. Set an acquisition price from current buyer evidence only when an exceptional sale process is consciously approved.
