## Short generic positioning
Compliance Drift Detector (CDD) is a local deterministic checkpoint engine that compares **structured policy claims** with **supplied, pre-classified operating evidence**. It distinguishes currently aligned controls that never drifted from those that recovered after an earlier breach, provides JSON/Markdown/HTML exports and supports independent SHA-256 artifact consistency checking. **It does not autonomously parse raw logs or certify compliance.**

We're exploring **commercial licensing, OEM embedding and paid technical evaluations**, **not an outright acquisition of the IP**. Enterprise/OEM terms are custom scoped; publicly posted individual subscriptions are not OEM pricing.
