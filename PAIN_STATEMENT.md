# Pain Statement

## The 30-Second Version

**Who feels this:** Compliance officers, engineering VPs, CISOs at companies with written policies that are supposed to govern system behavior — but nobody systematically checks across checkpoints whether reality still matches policy.

**The pain:** Your policy says one thing. Your system does another. The gap grows silently for months. You discover it during an audit, a breach, or a lawsuit — never proactively. By then, it may be difficult to establish the earliest observed checkpoint where the supplied evidence diverged from policy.

**What this gives you:** Checkpoint-based drift measurement between policy declarations and system behavior. Every policy claim is scored against supplied evidence. Drift trends are visible over time. When behavior diverges from policy, it is flagged with tamper-evident evidence of the earliest observed divergence in the supplied checkpoints.

**The artifact:** A downloadable drift report that shows: which policies are being followed, which are drifting, which are violated, and which system behaviors have no policy at all. Hash-sealed for tamper evidence.

## Why This Matters Now

- EU AI Act Article 9 requires risk management systems to be maintained — not just established
- ISO 27001 auditors test for "continual improvement" — which means you need evidence of alignment over time
- SOC 2 Type II covers a period, not a moment — drift during the period is a finding
- Insurance renewals increasingly require proof of governance consistency
- Regulators are shifting from "do you have a policy?" to "does your system follow it?"

Compliance Drift Detector fills the gap between "we have policies" and "we follow them."
