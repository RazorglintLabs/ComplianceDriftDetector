## Included interpretation scope

Each interpretation covers **one specific question about one previously generated, properly redacted CDD report**, using an excerpt of **up to 10 policy claims or five pages**. It does not cover a full dataset audit, independent validation of `compliant` labels, policy authoring or regulatory compliance conclusions.

No onsite assistance, live calls, custom integrations, client-by-client unlimited advice, bespoke investigations, unlimited data preparation, or guarantees of correction are included. Requests that exceed monthly caps may be quoted separately **only with prior written approval**. Review [support terms and detailed definitions](docs/SUPPORT_SCOPE.md).

## Product Boundaries

CDD deterministically compares structured claims and operator-supplied, **pre-classified** evidence across checkpoints. It does not parse natural-language policies, infer compliance from raw logs, or certify compliance. The included v2 JSON verifier performs **unkeyed hash consistency checks**, not authenticated origin verification.
